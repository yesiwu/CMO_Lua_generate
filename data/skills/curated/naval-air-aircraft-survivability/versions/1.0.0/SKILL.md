# Naval-Air Aircraft Survivability

## Quick Reference
- Preserve the aircraft's ability to deliver weapons before optimizing pursuit or repeated exposure.
- Treat loss reduction as a tactical objective only while preserving an actual strike opportunity.

## When To Use
- Use when carrier aircraft face layered surface-air defenses.
- Use when prior runs show aircraft losses before meaningful attack activity.

## Strategy Patterns
### Exposure Management
- Prefer an ingress and pop-up profile that minimizes predictable exposure while leaving a viable attack and return path.
- Compare one air-tactics change at a time before combining multiple changes.

## Counterexamples
- Do not remove or indefinitely delay the air strike merely to reduce losses.
- Do not infer weapon release or target damage without direct evidence.
