# Naval-Air Ammunition Reserve

## Quick Reference
- Balance a decisive first wave against the ability to respond after observed results.
- Express reserve policy through controlled fire quantity and reserve fields only.

## When To Use
- Use when a target is repeatedly damaged but not reliably neutralized.
- Use when the strike force has limited anti-ship ammunition across multiple targets.

## Strategy Patterns
### Deliberate Reserve
- Allocate ammunition according to target priority and expected need for follow-up fire.
- Keep reserve decisions consistent with the planned target sequence.

## Counterexamples
- Do not preserve reserve merely by weakening the only credible initial attack.
- Do not assume reserve is useful when there is no viable later attack opportunity.
