# Naval-Air Launch Sequencing

## Quick Reference
- Use launch delay to test temporal coordination between aircraft and surface operations.
- Keep the aircraft's assigned target and strike mission intact while evaluating timing.

## When To Use
- Use when aircraft arrive before surface pressure begins or after the tactical opportunity has changed.
- Use when prior runs show poor synchronization between platform types.

## Strategy Patterns
### Coordinated Start Time
- Adjust launch delay as a controlled variable against a stable surface-strike baseline.
- Compare whether the change preserves target damage while affecting aircraft survival or attack execution.

## Counterexamples
- Do not delay launch so long that the air operation no longer contributes to the planned strike.
- Do not change launch delay and target assignment together unless explicitly testing their interaction.
