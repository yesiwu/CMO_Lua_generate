# Naval-Air Salvo Timing

## Quick Reference
- Treat attack delay as an experiment in sequencing, not as an isolated cosmetic value.
- Preserve a coherent relationship between surface suppression and aircraft arrival.

## When To Use
- Use when multiple attacks can arrive simultaneously or in an unintended order.
- Use when defensive reactions may be shaped by the timing of earlier salvos.

## Strategy Patterns
### Sequenced Pressure
- Use distinct but purposeful attack timing to test whether staggered pressure improves the overall strike opportunity.
- Change timing without silently changing target assignment or ammunition policy unless testing an explicit combined hypothesis.

## Counterexamples
- Do not stagger attacks so widely that mutually supporting pressure disappears.
- Do not attribute an outcome solely to timing when target allocation changed at the same time.
