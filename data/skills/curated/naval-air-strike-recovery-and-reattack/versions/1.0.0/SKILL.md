# Naval-Air Strike Recovery And Reattack

## Quick Reference
- Preserve a viable return path and remaining combat utility after the initial attack attempt.
- Use recovery and re-attack as outcome measures, not as a reason to abandon the mission.

## When To Use
- Use when aircraft complete part of an attack chain but fail to return or contribute later.
- Use when ammunition reserve and sortie survivability must be considered together.

## Strategy Patterns
### Recoverable Strike Design
- Balance the initial strike with a feasible return and potential follow-up action.
- Use observed aircraft terminal state and remaining target damage to choose the next controlled experiment.

## Counterexamples
- Do not infer a successful recovery from missing telemetry.
- Do not preserve aircraft at the cost of eliminating all meaningful strike activity.
