# Naval-Air Surface-Air Coordination

## Quick Reference
- Coordinate target ownership, timing, and ammunition policy across platform types.
- Prefer interpretable changes that preserve a shared operational objective.

## When To Use
- Use when surface attacks and sorties can interfere, duplicate effort, or expose aircraft without mutual benefit.
- Use when the strike plan needs a coherent division of labor across ships and aircraft.

## Strategy Patterns
### Complementary Roles
- Use surface combatants to shape target pressure while aircraft pursue their assigned high-value or complementary target.
- Ensure every sortie target is consistent with the corresponding air attack objective.

## Counterexamples
- Do not assume simultaneous surface and air attacks are coordinated without checking actual timing and target allocation.
- Do not remove air operations solely to make loss statistics look better.
