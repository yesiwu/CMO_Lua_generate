# Naval-Air Target Deconfliction

## Quick Reference
- Give each attacking platform or strike group a clear primary target.
- Reserve duplicate fires for deliberate follow-up or confirmed re-attack needs.

## When To Use
- Use when multiple red surface and air operations attack the same enemy surface group.
- Use when ammunition is limited and target coverage matters more than redundant first strikes.

## Strategy Patterns
### Primary Target Ownership
- Assign high-value targets, escorts, and follow-up targets deliberately across surface and air operations.
- Keep sortie targets aligned with their corresponding air attack objective.

## Counterexamples
- Do not prohibit coordinated saturation when a single protected target is the operational priority.
- Do not assume two attacks are redundant without checking their timing and intended weapon effect.
